The Moon is smaller than the Earth and has less mass, so its pull is weaker. At the surface, gravity on the Moon is **1.62 metres per second squared**. On Earth it is **9.80**. That is almost exactly one-sixth.

So a person who weighs **88 pounds** on Earth would weigh about **15 pounds** on the Moon. The person is still the same person, with the same amount of stuff in them. Only the pull has changed.

This is why the astronauts who walked on the Moon could jump so far, and why it felt so easy to lift a heavy suitcase there.

> **Look closer:** Your **mass** would stay the same on the Moon. Only your **weight** would change.
