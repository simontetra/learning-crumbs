*The Dark Side of the Moon* was released in March 1973 by Capitol Records in the United States. It was certified gold in the US by April 1973, and it entered the US chart in July that year.

The article says it ranks seventh among the best-selling albums of all time in the UK, but it gives no unit figures or source for that ranking. It also gives a chart run of 736 weeks, but it refers to the Billboard Hot 100, which tracks singles, so that figure needs checking against the album chart.

In 2013 the album was selected for the US National Recording Registry by the Library of Congress.

> **Look closer:** A chart record depends on which chart is named. An album and a single are measured on different lists, so check the chart before you compare two records.
