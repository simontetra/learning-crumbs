*The Phantom of the Opera* opened on Broadway in 1988, at the Majestic Theatre. The music is by Andrew Lloyd Webber, the lyrics by Charles Hart and Richard Stilgoe, and the book by Stilgoe and Lloyd Webber. It is based on Gaston Leroux's 1910 novel.

It ran until 2023, and the New York Theatre Guide describes it as the longest-running show in Broadway history.

> **Look closer:** The New York Theatre Guide article was written in 2022, and gave February 2023 as the closing date. Its run ended in 2023, so the record is a matter of history rather than of the present day.
