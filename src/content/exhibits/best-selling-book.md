The Bible is widely described as the best-selling book in history. Guinness World Records gave an estimate of 5 billion copies, as of 1995.

The number is hard to pin down. Many publishers have printed the Bible over many centuries, and early sales were often estimates from booksellers and publishers.

For books that are not religious, the Harry Potter series is said to have sold more than 600 million copies, but that is a whole series, not one book.

> **Look closer:** A Bible printed by a press in one century might be sold again by a different printer a hundred years later. Counting copies is harder than it sounds.
