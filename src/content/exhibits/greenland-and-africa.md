On the Mercator map, Greenland looks almost the same size as Africa. Put the two side by side on a globe, and the picture changes completely.

Africa covers about 30.4 million square kilometres of land. Greenland covers about 2.2 million. Africa is roughly fourteen times bigger.

The map does this to anything far from the equator. Countries near the top and bottom of the page look bigger than they are, and countries near the middle, many of them in Africa and South America, look smaller. Europe and North America are not really bigger than they look on the map, but they look bigger compared with their neighbours near the equator.

> **Look closer:** If you cut out Greenland and laid it over Africa, it would fit with room to spare. The map can't show that, because it is stretching one of them.
