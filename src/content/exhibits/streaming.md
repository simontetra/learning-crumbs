For more than a hundred years, to play a piece of music you needed a **copy** of it: a cylinder, a record, a tape, a CD, or a file.

**Streaming** changed that. When you stream a song, it travels to your phone from a computer far away, a little at a time, and plays as it arrives. Nothing is kept. Next time, it is sent again.

Streaming needs fast internet and clever compression, like the tricks that made the MP3. By the 2000s both were ready. In **Stockholm**, Sweden, **Daniel Ek** and **Martin Lorentzon** founded **Spotify** in **2006**, and it launched in several European countries on **7 October 2008**.

Today Spotify has well over **100 million** songs, and hundreds of millions of people use it every month. Many other streaming services do the same.

> **Look closer:** If you played every song on Spotify one after another, day and night without stopping, it would take more than 600 years.
