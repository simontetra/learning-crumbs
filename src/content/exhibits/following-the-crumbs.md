Hansel and Gretel are two children whose parents leave them in the forest because there is not enough food. The **Brothers Grimm** published the story in 1812, in their collection *Children's and Household Tales*.

In that first edition, the cruel parent is the children's own mother. She is called a stepmother from the 1840 edition onwards.

Hansel prepares for the journey both times. The first time, he drops **white pebbles**, which shine in the moonlight and guide them home. The second time, the door is locked, so he crumbles bread and drops it along the path. The birds eat every crumb, and the children are lost. After three days, a dove leads them to a house made of bread, with a cake roof and sugar windows.

> **Look closer:** The pebbles worked. The crumbs did not.
