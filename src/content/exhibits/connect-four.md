Connect Four was invented by two American game designers, Howard Wexler and Ned Strongin. Milton Bradley launched it in 1974, and it sold widely from the first year.

The game looks simple, but it has a definite answer. In 1988 Victor Allis and James D. Allen, working separately, proved that the player who goes first can always win if they play perfectly.

> **Look closer:** A game that seems to have endless possibilities can still have a known answer. It took two people working independently to find it.
