During sleep the body does a lot of maintenance work.

- **Tissue repair:** the body builds muscle, repairs damage and regenerates cells.
- **Growth hormone:** the hormone is released in its highest amounts during sleep, and it drives repair and, in children, growth of bone and muscle.
- **Brain waste:** the brain clears toxins that build up while we are awake, mostly during deep sleep.
- **Immunity:** non-REM sleep is especially important for the immune system, and the body makes more infection-fighting proteins, called cytokines, when we are ill.
- **Memory:** sleep helps the brain fix memories, and REM sleep plays a part in this.
- **Blood sugar:** deep sleep helps the body's cells respond to insulin.

> **Look closer:** Your heart rate, breathing and body temperature all drop during sleep, which saves energy for the repair work.
