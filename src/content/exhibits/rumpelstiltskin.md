A miller boasts to the king that his daughter can spin straw into gold. The king has her locked in a room full of straw and tells her to spin it into gold by morning, or she will die.

A little imp appears and spins the straw for her, in exchange for her necklace, and then her ring. On the third night she has nothing left to give, so she promises her first child. The king marries her, and the imp's price is forgotten, until the queen has a baby.

The imp comes back to claim the child. He will let her keep it if she can guess his name within three days. She tries name after name. On the last day, she overhears him singing his name in the woods, and she says it.

> **Look closer:** In the 1812 edition, the imp runs off angrily and never comes back. In the 1857 edition, he tears himself in two.
