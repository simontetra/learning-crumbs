The Beatles are one of the most famous bands ever, and many sales figures are quoted for them. Some of these figures are hard to check.

Press reports said the Beatles had passed a billion units by 1984. That figure used a weighted "discs sold" unit that included solo work, so it is misleading as a count of the Beatles alone. A figure of more than 600 million albums is quoted, but without a source or a year.

*Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band* is given at 5.4 million UK units by the Official Charts Company, without a year. An independent estimate puts it at about 3.55 million.

> **Look closer:** Music sales figures depend on what is counted, such as albums, singles, weighted units or solo work. Comparing bands is only fair when they are counted in the same way.
