*Evita* opened in London at the Prince Edward Theatre on 21 June 1978. Its subject is Eva Perón, the second wife of Argentine president Juan Perón.

Tim Rice had the idea for the show, and Andrew Lloyd Webber agreed to write the melodies. The Broadway production opened on 25 September 1979, and went on to win seven Tony Awards, including Best Musical and Best Score.

> **Look closer:** One source gives 1978 for the London opening, and another snippet gave 1979. The London Theatre page gives 21 June 1978, so that is the date used here. The Broadway theatre is not confirmed.
