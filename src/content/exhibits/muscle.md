Bend your arm and squeeze. See the bump that moves under your skin?

The Romans looked at that and saw a **mouse**. Their word for mouse was *mus*, and *musculus* meant **little mouse**. They used the same word for a muscle, perhaps because a muscle rippling under the skin looked like a small mouse scurrying beneath a cloth, and a bulging muscle is a little mouse-shaped too.

The word travelled from Latin into French and then into English, and we still use it. So every time you flex your muscles, in Latin you are showing off your little mice.

> **Look closer:** It isn't only English. In Greek, the word *mys* also meant both mouse and muscle. People in different places noticed the same thing.
