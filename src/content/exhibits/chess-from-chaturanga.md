The oldest known ancestor of chess is **chaturanga**, which was played in northern India in the 6th century. The name is often given as meaning "four divisions", a reference to the four parts of an army.

The game quickly spread west. In Persia it became **shatranj**. It reached the Arab world in the 8th century, carried by the expansion of Islam, and reached Europe by about the 9th or 10th century.

The pieces changed as the game travelled. The queen gained her modern power, moving any number of squares in a straight line, around the 15th century, in Spain and Italy.

> **Look closer:** The history of chess is pieced together from few early sources, so the dates above are approximate. Some accounts differ by a century.
