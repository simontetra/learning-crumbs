*The Lion King* opened on Broadway at the New Amsterdam Theatre on 13 November 1997. It is based on Disney's animated film of 1994.

The director was Julie Taymor, who won the 1998 Tony Award for Best Direction. She was the first woman to win that award. The show moved to the Minskoff Theatre in 2006, where it still plays.

Its run is one of the longest on Broadway, and it is often described as one of the highest-grossing shows of all time.

> **Look closer:** Records like "highest-grossing" change as new figures come in, so the claim is only as current as the source. The ET Online article was written for the show's twentieth anniversary.
