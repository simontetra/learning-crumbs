Missing sleep affects the brain and body in several ways.

- **Attention and memory:** concentration drops, reaction times slow, and it becomes harder to learn new information or form long-term memories. Recovery can take days, even when you no longer feel tired.
- **Mood:** irritability and moodiness the day after poor sleep. Chronic poor sleep is linked to depression and anxiety, and the link runs both ways.
- **Balance and movement:** coordination and balance get worse, raising the risk of falls and accidents, including car accidents.
- **Immunity:** colds and flu are more likely, and recovery is slower.
- **Heart and metabolism:** a higher risk of high blood pressure, and of diabetes and heart disease in the long term.
- **Brain health over a lifetime:** long-term sleep deprivation may raise the risk of cognitive decline and dementia. Clearing waste from the brain is harder without enough deep sleep.

> **Look closer:** A single bad night is recoverable. Repeated short nights add up, which is why the age ranges in *How much sleep do we need?* matter.
