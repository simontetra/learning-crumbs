Queen formed in London. Brian May and Roger Taylor were joined by Freddie Mercury, and the band played its first gig under the name Queen in July 1970. John Deacon joined as bassist in March 1971.

On 13 July 1985 they played Live Aid at Wembley Stadium. The article cites a stadium crowd of 72,000 and a television audience of 1.9 billion. A 2005 poll ranked the performance the greatest rock performance of all time, though the source does not name the pollster.

Their *Greatest Hits* album (1981) is the best-selling album in UK history, with 6 million copies sold by 2014. Freddie Mercury died on 24 November 1991.

> **Look closer:** The Live Aid audience figure is a television estimate, and the stadium crowd is a count on the day. Both are big numbers, but they measure different things.
