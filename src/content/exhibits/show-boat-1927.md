*Show Boat* opened on Broadway in 1927. It was produced by Florenz Ziegfeld, with music by Jerome Kern and book and lyrics by Oscar Hammerstein II, and it was based on Edna Ferber's 1926 novel.

It is called the first completely integrated musical, because the singing, dancing and acting all carry the story, rather than the songs sitting between the scenes.

It also took on serious, dark themes, including interracial marriage and its legal consequences. Its Black characters were unusually complex and sympathetic for the time, and the show set the standard for the musicals that followed.

> **Look closer:** Songs from the show became standards that have been recorded by many artists since.
