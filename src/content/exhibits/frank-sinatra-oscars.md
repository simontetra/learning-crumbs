Frank Sinatra was born on 12 December 1915 in Hoboken, New Jersey. In 1940 he joined Tommy Dorsey's band, and his solo career took off in 1943.

In 1945 he received a Special Academy Award for *The House I Live In*. In 1953 he won the Best Supporting Actor Oscar for *From Here to Eternity*.

He signed with Capitol Records in 1953 and later left to found Reprise Records. In 1966 "Strangers in the Night" reached No. 1 and won the Grammy for Record of the Year. He died on 14 May 1998 in Los Angeles, California.

> **Look closer:** Sinatra was honoured as a singer and as an actor. The two Oscars came eight years apart, for two very different kinds of work.
