Madonna was born on 16 August 1958 in Bay City, Michigan. Her first major album, *Madonna*, came out in 1983.

The next year, *Like a Virgin* (1984) became the first album by a female artist to sell more than five million copies in the United States. In 2015 Guinness World Records named her the best-selling female artist of all time.

> **Look closer:** Sales claims depend on what is counted, such as albums, singles or weighted units. A "best-selling" title is a claim made under particular rules, so it is worth asking who made it and how.
