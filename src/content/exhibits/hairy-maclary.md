Every picture in every book was drawn by somebody. For Hairy Maclary, and for Schnitzel von Krumm, Hercules Morse and Scarface Claw, that somebody is **Lynley Dodd**.

She was born in **1941** in Rotorua, New Zealand. She was an only child who loved words. She studied at the Elam School of Art in Auckland and then taught art at Queen Margaret College in Wellington for five years.

Her first picture book, in **1973**, was *My Cat Likes to Hide in Boxes*, written by Eve Sutton, a member of her family. Lynley did the pictures. Ten years later, her publisher asked her for a new story in a hurry. She looked in the notebook where she kept ideas, and out fell a page with a short rhyme and a sketch of a scruffy little dog. *Hairy Maclary from Donaldson's Dairy* came out in **1983**.

She works out the characters first, then the words, and only then makes rough drawings. Some stories took her up to 25 drafts to get the rhythm right, because, she says, if the words don't hang together nicely, the book won't work. At first Hairy Maclary was "a bit squat"; over the years he grew more elegant, with flowing hair.

She went on to make more than thirty books, and in New Zealand she became Dame Lynley Dodd.

> **Look closer:** So the answer to the question at the door of this room is: a woman with a notebook of ideas and an ear for rhyme, on the other side of the world. Every book on your shelf has someone like her behind it.
