Nina Simone was born Eunice Kathleen Waymon on 21 February 1933 in Tryon, North Carolina. Her first album, *Little Girl Blue*, was recorded in 1957 and released in 1959.

In 1963 she wrote and released "Mississippi Goddam". It was a response to the assassination of the civil rights campaigner Medgar Evers and to the bombing of the 16th Street Baptist Church in Birmingham, Alabama.

She left the United States in 1973 and later lived in Liberia and in Europe. She died on 21 April 2003 at her home in Carry-le-Rouet, France.

> **Look closer:** A song can be a record of its time. "Mississippi Goddam" was written in anger at events that were still being reported, and it is still listened to as a protest.
