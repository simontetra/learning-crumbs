On 29 June 1613, during a performance of *Henry VIII*, a fire broke out in the theatre. It spread to the thatched roof and destroyed the building.

The theatre was rebuilt, and the company carried on performing.

> **Look closer:** Fire was a real danger to early playhouses, which were built of timber and had thatched roofs.
