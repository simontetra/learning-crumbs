The Goldilocks zone is a nickname for the **habitable zone**: the band of distances from a star where a planet is neither too hot nor too cold for **liquid water** to sit on its surface.

Where a planet sits in that band depends mainly on how far it is from its star. A planet's size and the kind of star also matter. Earth is "just right". If Earth were as far out as Pluto, its oceans would freeze. If it were as close as Mercury, its water would boil away.

> **Look closer:** The same Sun shines on every planet. What decides whether water is ice, liquid or steam is how far the planet is from it.
