JVC launched the VHS tape in the late 1970s, and it was the first format that let most people record television and watch films at home.

For a while VHS had a rival, Betamax, in what became known as the format war. By 1987 the American video recorder market was based on VHS, and it stayed dominant until digital discs arrived about a decade later.

> **Look closer:** Home recording meant that people could watch what they wanted, when they wanted, for the first time.
