*My son was telling me about this one: the Lamborghini and Ferrari feud over a clutch.*

After the Second World War, an Italian mechanic called **Ferruccio Lamborghini** started building **tractors** out of leftover army machinery. Farmers needed them, and he grew rich.

He liked fast cars, and he owned several **Ferraris**. But one of them kept having trouble with its **clutch**, and he had to keep taking it back to the Ferrari factory to be fixed.

What happened next is a famous story. As Lamborghini told it, he finally went to see **Enzo Ferrari** himself and told him his cars were rubbish. Ferrari replied that Lamborghini might be able to drive a tractor, but he would never be able to handle a Ferrari properly.

Whether those were the exact words, nobody can say: historians point out that only the two men knew what was really said. But we do know what Lamborghini did next. In **1963** he founded **Automobili Lamborghini**, to build grand touring cars good enough to beat Ferrari's. That same year he showed his first prototype at the Turin Motor Show.

Today Lamborghini and Ferrari are two of the most famous **supercar** makers in the world, still rivals, still in the north of Italy, not far from each other.

> **Look closer:** Lamborghini tractors are still made too. A grumpy customer started one of the most famous car names on Earth.
