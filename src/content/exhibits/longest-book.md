Venmurasu is a novel by the Tamil writer Jeyamohan. It has 26 volumes, based on the Mahabharata, and was completed in 2020. By word count it is the longest novel the list records, at about 3,640,000 words.

Other books run close, but they are counted differently. Artamène, a French novel from 1649 to 1653, is listed at about 13,095 pages, and its authorship is disputed. Pages depend on the size of the page and the type, so page counts are only a rough guide.

> **Look closer:** When someone says "the longest book", ask how they counted it. Words, pages and volumes can all give different winners.
