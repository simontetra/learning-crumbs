David Bowie was born David Robert Jones on 8 January 1947 in Brixton, south London. He is a solo singer, but he worked with a band, the Spiders from Mars, on his 1972 album.

*The Rise and Fall of Ziggy Stardust and the Spiders from Mars* was released in June 1972. It reached No. 3 on the UK album chart. The album was built around the character Ziggy Stardust.

Later milestones include "Fame", his first US number one single in 1975, and "Let's Dance", which reached number one in the US and UK in 1983. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1996. He died of liver cancer at his New York home on 10 January 2016.

> **Look closer:** A solo name can hide a band. Bowie's records were often made with other musicians, and this room counts him as a singer because he was the one out front.
