David Bowie was born David Robert Jones on 8 January 1947 in Brixton, south London. He made his name as a solo singer, and on his breakthrough album he was backed by a band, the Spiders from Mars.

*The Rise and Fall of Ziggy Stardust and the Spiders from Mars* came out in 1972. The album was built around the character Ziggy Stardust, and the Spiders played behind him.

Later milestones include "Let's Dance", released in 1983, and his induction into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1996. He died in New York on 10 January 2016.

> **Look closer:** Bowie's records were often made with other musicians. This room counts him as a solo singer because he was the one the audience came to see.
