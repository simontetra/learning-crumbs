In February 1943 a farmer, **Dionisio Pulido**, and his wife Paula were burning shrubs in their cornfield in Michoacán, in central Mexico. Under the field, the ground was opening. A **fissure** split the earth, and a volcano began to grow.

Within a day it had built a **cone** 50 metres high. Within a week it was 100 metres. Over the following months, **lava** poured out and buried the villages of **Paricutín** and **San Juan Parangaricutiro**. Scientists came from all over the world to watch it grow.

The eruption ended in **1952**. The cone had reached **424 metres**: a mountain that had not existed in the lifetime of the farmer's children.

> **Look closer:** Parícutin is one of the best-studied volcanoes in the world. Geologists came from many countries to watch it grow.
