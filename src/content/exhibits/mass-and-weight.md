Mass and weight are not the same thing, even though people use the words as if they were.

**Mass** is the amount of matter an object contains. It is measured in **kilograms**, and it stays the same wherever you take the object.

**Weight** is the pull that gravity exerts on that mass. It is a force, measured in **newtons** by scientists. Because gravity is different in different places, weight changes too.

So a bag of sugar has the same mass on the Moon as it does on Earth. Only its weight is different.

> **Look closer:** When you step on the bathroom scales, you are measuring the pull of gravity on your mass, which is why the number on the scales can change depending on where you are.
