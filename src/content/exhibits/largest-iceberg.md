The largest iceberg on record is **Iceberg B-15**, which broke off the Ross Ice Shelf in Antarctica in 2000. WorldAtlas gives its area as about **11,007 square kilometres**.

Guinness World Records now lists **D15A** as the largest iceberg. It broke off the West Ice Shelf in January 2016, and Guinness gives its area as about **3,050 square kilometres** in April 2026. It took the title after the iceberg A23A broke up in late 2025.

The two records are different because icebergs break up, and the title moves to whatever is largest at the time. The area of an iceberg also depends on how it is measured.

> **Look closer:** Most of an iceberg's bulk is under the water, and that hidden part is one reason icebergs are dangerous to ships.
