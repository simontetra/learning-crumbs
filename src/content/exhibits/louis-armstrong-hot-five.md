Louis Armstrong was born on 4 August 1901 in New Orleans, Louisiana. He learned the cornet at a home for boys in 1912, and in 1922 he moved to Chicago to join King Oliver's Creole Jazz Band.

As "Louis Armstrong and His Hot Five", he recorded in Chicago from 1925 to 1928, making over 60 records. In 1937 he became the first African-American entertainer to host a nationwide radio show.

In 1967 he released "What a Wonderful World", a vocal ballad without trumpet. It reached No. 1 and became his longest-lasting song. He died on 6 July 1971 at his home in Queens, New York.

> **Look closer:** The Hot Five recordings were made in a small group, but they changed how jazz soloists were heard. Many of the records were made in a few short years.
