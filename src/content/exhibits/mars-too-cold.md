Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun, and it is much colder than Earth. Its average surface temperature is about **−80 °F (about −63 °C)**. Sources give slightly different averages, depending on how they were measured.

Mars has an atmosphere, but it is thin, and it does not hold much heat. Its average temperature is far below freezing, so water on its surface cannot stay liquid for long.

> **Look closer:** Venus is too hot and Mars is too cold. Earth lies between them, and it is the one with the right amount of warmth for oceans.
