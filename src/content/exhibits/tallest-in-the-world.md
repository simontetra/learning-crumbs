The Eiffel Tower was completed in March 1889. At that time it stood about 300 metres (985 feet) high, and it was the tallest structure in the world. It kept that title until the Chrysler Building in New York City was completed in 1930, a period of 41 years.

> **Look closer:** A record for the tallest structure lasts only until someone builds higher. Today the Eiffel Tower's height depends on whether you count its antennas, which take it to about 324 metres.
