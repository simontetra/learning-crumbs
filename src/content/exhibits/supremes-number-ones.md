The Supremes began in Detroit in 1959 as the Primettes, a sister act to the Primes. They signed to Motown in January 1961.

They had 12 Billboard Hot 100 number one singles. The source calls them America's most successful vocal group. Their hits include "Baby Love".

The line-up changed many times. Diana Ross & the Supremes gave their last performance together on 14 January 1970 in Las Vegas. Ross then left for a solo career, and the group carried on with new members until it disbanded in 1977.

> **Look closer:** The group's name changed as its line-up did, from The Supremes to Diana Ross & the Supremes and back again. The number ones belong to the group, so the names tell you who was singing at the time.
