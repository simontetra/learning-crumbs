Ofcom's Media Nations report for 2026 measures what people watched in 2025. On average, each person in the UK watched four hours and 23 minutes of video in the home each day, on any device.

Broadcast television still made up 2 hours and 19 minutes of that, or 53 percent. Streaming on services such as Netflix made up 42 minutes. YouTube and other video-sharing sites took 51 minutes, with YouTube alone at 41 minutes, up 18 percent on the year before.

Netflix accounted for 9 percent of in-home viewing, and 88 percent of all viewing took place on a television set.

> **Look closer:** Broadcast viewing is falling, while viewing through the internet is growing. The television is still the main screen, but more of what it shows now comes through the internet.
