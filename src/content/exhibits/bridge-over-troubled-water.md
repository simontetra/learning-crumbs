*Bridge Over Troubled Water* was released in 1970. It became Simon & Garfunkel's most successful album, and the source notes that it was also their last studio album.

At the 1970 Grammy Awards the duo won six awards, and five of them were attributed to the title track. The article does not name the ceremony, so the year is taken from the 1970 release.

The source does not give a formation date, or a year for the duo's split.

> **Look closer:** Several awards can go to one song, and an album can win for its title track, its performance and its production. Read the award list before you count the wins.
