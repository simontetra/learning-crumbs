Counting books is hard. Estimates depend on which records are used, and not every book is registered.

One estimate, from Mental Floss in 2016, gave about 755,755 new titles a year, based on recent production. Another, using ISBN registrations for 2011 to 2013, gave about 2.27 million.

A count made by Google Books in 2010 found about 130 million books in total.

> **Look closer:** If you read one book a day, the books published in a single year would keep you going for about 2,000 years at the lower estimate, and about 6,000 at the higher one.
