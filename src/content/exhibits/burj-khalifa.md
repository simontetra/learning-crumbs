The Burj Khalifa in Dubai is 828 metres tall. It has 163 floors above ground and one floor below. It was completed in 2010 and formally opened on 4 January 2010, although its interior was not fully finished at that time.

Heights of tall buildings are measured in different ways. The tower's highest point, including its antennae and equipment, is about 830 metres.

> **Look closer:** At 828 metres, the Burj Khalifa is nearly three times as tall as the Eiffel Tower was when it was completed, at about 300 metres.
