Ella Fitzgerald was born on 25 April 1917 in Newport News, Virginia. She made her name in the 1930s, and her 1938 recording of "A-Tisket, A-Tasket" brought her wide public acclaim.

Between 1956 and 1964 she recorded eight Songbook albums for Verve Records. Each one was devoted to the songs of a single songwriter or songwriting partnership: Cole Porter, Rodgers and Hart, Duke Ellington, Irving Berlin, George and Ira Gershwin, Harold Arlen, Jerome Kern and Johnny Mercer.

She won 13 Grammy Awards. She died on 15 June 1996 in Beverly Hills, California.

> **Look closer:** The Songbooks were a way of recording the great songs of American popular music. Each album's title names the composer, so the shelf works as a list of who wrote the songs.
