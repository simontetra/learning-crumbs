The Rolling Stones were formed in London in 1962. Brian Jones started the band, with the pianist Ian Stewart. Mick Jagger and Keith Richards later led the songwriting, and Charlie Watts and Bill Wyman completed the early lineup.

Their first UK number one was "It's All Over Now", a cover, in June 1964.

Brian Jones died on 3 July 1969. About two days later the band played a free concert in Hyde Park, London. The article estimates the crowd at 250,000. Mick Jagger read from Shelley's *Adonais* and released butterflies in Jones's memory.

> **Look closer:** The article claims the Stones have sold "more than 200 million albums worldwide", but it gives no source for that figure. It is a claim to check, not a fact yet.
