Even at the height of the space station, gravity is still strong. It is **about 90 per cent** of the gravity on the ground.

So why do the astronauts float? Because they are in **free fall**. The station is falling towards the Earth, and everything inside it falls at the same rate. Nothing pushes up against the astronauts' feet, so they feel weightless.

The station also moves sideways very fast. As it drops, the curve of the Earth drops away beneath it, so it keeps circling the planet instead of landing.

> **Look closer:** A passenger in a lift whose cable has snapped would float in the same way for a moment, because the lift and the passenger fall together.
