The Diamond Sutra is a Buddhist text, and it is the oldest printed book that still has a date. Its colophon, the note at the end, records that it was finished on 11 May 868.

It was printed from carved wooden blocks, one for the picture and others for the text, which were pressed onto paper. The printer, Wang Jie, made it for his parents and for all living beings.

It was found in 1900, during restoration work at a Buddhist monastery near Dunhuang, in north-west China.

> **Look closer:** The printer wrote that the book was made for free distribution. Nobody had to pay for it, which is quite a thing to write on a book in 868.
