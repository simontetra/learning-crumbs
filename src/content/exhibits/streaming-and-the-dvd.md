Netflix began in 1998 as a rental service for DVDs, which came to the door by post. Discs replaced the video tape as the way to watch films at home.

On 16 January 2007 it launched streaming, with about 1,000 titles. These were roughly one per cent of its library of around 70,000 discs. Among the first films was *Casablanca*, and the first programmes included the 1990 BBC series *House of Cards*.

> **Look closer:** The first streaming titles came from the same library as the discs. The change was in how they arrived, not in what was watched.
