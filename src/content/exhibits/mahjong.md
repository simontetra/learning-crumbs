Mahjong is a tile game for four players. It is usually dated to China in the mid-19th century, around the 1850s, and the earliest documented versions were played around Shanghai and other coastal cities.

In 1920 the American Joseph Babcock published a simplified set of rules for Americans. Mahjong then became a craze in America in the 1920s.

In 1937 the National Mah Jongg League was founded in America.

> **Look closer:** Many stories about mahjong are repeated without evidence, such as claims about the number of tiles and their meanings. The dates here are the ones the sources agree on.
