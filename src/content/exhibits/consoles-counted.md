These figures are estimates of how many consoles have been sold. By the end of December 2025, VGChartz put the PlayStation 5 at about 89.4 million, the Xbox Series X and S at about 34.2 million and the Nintendo Switch 2 at about 15.6 million.

The Switch 2 was launched in June 2025, and its first month was a record for Nintendo.

> **Look closer:** These are estimates, not official sales figures from the companies. Consoles that have been sold do not always mean that they are still being played.
