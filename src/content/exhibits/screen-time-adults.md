Adults in the UK spent an average of four hours and 30 minutes online each day in Ofcom's 2025 survey. That was ten minutes more than the year before.

Women spent more time online than men, at four hours and 43 minutes a day, which is 26 minutes more.

> **Look closer:** This is time online, which includes work, shopping, messages and games. It is not the same as time spent watching television.
