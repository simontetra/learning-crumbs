*Wicked* is a musical prequel to events in L. Frank Baum's *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz*, which was published in 1900. It also leads into the 1939 film. The music and lyrics are by Stephen Schwartz, and the book is by Winnie Holzman.

The show opened on Broadway at the Gershwin Theatre in October 2003, after a pre-Broadway run in San Francisco. The New York Theatre Guide says it has run at the Gershwin for nearly twenty years, and in 2023 called it the fourth-longest-running show on Broadway.
