Children today spend a large part of their day online. Ofcom's 2025 survey found that children aged 8 to 14 spend about three hours a day online, and that 13 and 14 year olds spend about four hours.

Within that time, YouTube takes the largest share for children, at 48 minutes a day. Snapchat takes 45 minutes.

> **Look closer:** These figures count time online, not all screen time. Television and other screens are measured separately.
