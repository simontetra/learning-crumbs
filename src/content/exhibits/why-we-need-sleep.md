Sleep is not a pause in the brain's work. During sleep, the brain helps store memories from the day. Research on people and animals suggests that **sleep helps memories settle in**, especially practical skills such as playing an instrument, which improve after a night's sleep.

Sleep also lets the brain recover. Scientists think it helps regain the energy that the brain uses during the day, and it may clear waste that builds up while we are awake.

The reviews are clear on the main point, but they say the details are still unresolved. Some researchers think sleep weakens connections the brain no longer needs, while others think the replay of memories during sleep does the work.

> **Look closer:** Sleep does not stop the day's learning. It works on it while we rest, which is why a night's sleep often helps before an exam.
