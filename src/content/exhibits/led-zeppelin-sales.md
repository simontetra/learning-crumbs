Led Zeppelin were formed in London in 1968 by Robert Plant, Jimmy Page, John Paul Jones and John Bonham. Their debut album, *Led Zeppelin*, came out in 1969. The band announced its break-up in December 1980, after the death of John Bonham.

The source gives worldwide sales of over 300 million records. It attributes the figure to Warner Music Germany, Billboard and Oricon charts. The debut album is said to have sold over 8 million copies, though no source is given for that figure.

The US figures do not agree. One part of the source gives 111.5 million certified units, while another gives over 115 million album sales. The page does not reconcile the two.

> **Look closer:** "Sold" can mean shipped, certified or estimated, and these are different things. When two figures for the same country differ, the honest answer is a range.
