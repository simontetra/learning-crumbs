Our skin is good at keeping water out of the body, but it is not completely waterproof. The outer layer soaks up a little.

The outer layer of skin is made largely of **keratin**, the same protein as hair and nails. When hands are soaked in water, the keratin absorbs it and swells. The inside of the fingers does not swell in the same way, so the outer layer ends up with too much material for the space, and it **wrinkles, like a gathered skirt**.

When the water dries, the outer layer shrinks back and the wrinkles go away. The skin is working as a barrier the whole time, just not a perfect one.

> **Look closer:** The wrinkles in the bath are a sign that the outside of your skin has taken in some of the water. Your skin was never meant to be a raincoat.
