*West Side Story* opened on Broadway at the Winter Garden Theatre on 26 September 1957. Its story takes Shakespeare's *Romeo and Juliet* and moves it to the streets of New York, where two gangs are at war.

Arthur Laurents wrote the book, Leonard Bernstein wrote the music, and Stephen Sondheim wrote the lyrics. Jerome Robbins had the original idea, and he also directed and choreographed the first production.

A film version came out in 1961, directed by Robert Wise and Robbins. Steven Spielberg directed a second film in 2021.

> **Look closer:** The show began as an idea of Jerome Robbins, and the Broadway version came before either film. The sources give the 1961 and 2021 film years, so those are safe to repeat.
