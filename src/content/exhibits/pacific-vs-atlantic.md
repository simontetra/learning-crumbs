The Pacific Ocean is the **biggest ocean on Earth**. Britannica gives its area as about **165 million square kilometres**, not counting the seas along its edges.

The Atlantic is the second-largest ocean. Live Science gives its area as about **106 million square kilometres**. Divide one by the other and the Pacific comes out at about one and a half times the size of the Atlantic.

The two figures above come from different sources, which is why both are given as approximate.

> **Look closer:** Find the Pacific on a map of the world, then find the Atlantic. The Pacific is wider than the Atlantic and takes up more of the map.
