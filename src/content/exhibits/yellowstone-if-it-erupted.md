Yellowstone is a volcano. It has had **three giant caldera-forming eruptions**, at about **2.1 million**, **1.3 million** and **640,000 years ago**. The last one formed the caldera that is there today.

The US Geological Survey says that a future eruption of this kind would have effects that are **worldwide**. Near the volcano, hot pyroclastic flows would reach parts of Montana, Idaho and Wyoming. Ash would fall across other parts of the United States, thinning out with distance. The climate would change for years to decades, because so much ash and gas would reach the air.

The USGS also says the chance of a caldera-forming eruption there in the next few thousand years is **exceedingly small**.

> **Look closer:** The biggest effects would be felt near the volcano and in the weather. The USGS describes them as worldwide, but it also says the chance of this kind of eruption in the next few thousand years is very small.
