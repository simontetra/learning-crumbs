*Les Misérables* is based on Victor Hugo's nineteenth-century novel. The French composers Claude-Michel Schönberg and Alain Boublil wrote the first version, and it was staged in a Paris sports arena in 1980. The production closed after three months.

The English-language version opened at the Barbican Centre in London on 8 October 1985, in a co-production with the Royal Shakespeare Company. It sold out its three-month run there, and moved to the West End that October. It opened on Broadway at the Imperial Theatre on 17 March 1987.

The Broadway run closed on 18 May 2003, after 6,680 performances. The show won eight Tony Awards, including Best Musical.

> **Look closer:** The Playbill history was written in 2014, when the London production was still running. Its later closing is not in this source, so check the current position before saying it has closed.
