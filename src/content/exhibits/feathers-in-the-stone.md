Dinosaurs with feathers are no longer a surprise. Scientists have found nearly **50 feathered species**, and most of the fossils come from **Liaoning Province** in north-east China.

Those fossils are from rocks dated to about 120 to 131 million years ago. The feathers on some of these dinosaurs were simple, bristle-like fuzz rather than the flight feathers of a modern bird.

Researchers suggest that some of these bristle-like feathers may have served a display function rather than flight.

> **Look closer:** Birds are living dinosaurs. The feathers on a robin in your garden are part of the same family history as the ones found in Liaoning.
