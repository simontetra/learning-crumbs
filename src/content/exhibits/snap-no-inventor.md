Snap is one of the simplest games there is. Players take turns turning over cards, and the first person to shout "Snap" when two cards of the same rank appear together wins the pile.

No single person is credited with inventing it. It most likely grew out of informal family play with an ordinary pack of cards in Britain. The pack itself has been documented in Europe since at least the 14th century.

Card games for children became popular in Britain during the Victorian period, and Snap probably took its familiar form in the second half of the 19th century.

> **Look closer:** A game that nobody invented is hard to trace to a single date. Snap has simply been played for so long that it belongs to everyone.
