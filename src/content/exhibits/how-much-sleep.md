The National Sleep Foundation gives these ranges for hours of sleep in a 24-hour day:

- **Newborns (0 to 3 months):** 14 to 17 hours
- **Infants (4 to 11 months):** 12 to 15 hours
- **Toddlers (1 to 2 years):** 11 to 14 hours
- **Preschoolers (3 to 5 years):** 10 to 13 hours
- **School-age children (6 to 13 years):** 9 to 11 hours
- **Teenagers (14 to 17 years):** 8 to 10 hours
- **Younger adults (18 to 25 years):** 7 to 9 hours
- **Adults (26 to 64 years):** 7 to 9 hours
- **Older adults (65 and over):** 7 to 8 hours

The Foundation's recommendations were drawn up by an 18-scientist panel reviewing the research. A range is a guide, not a target. Some people need more or less than the range.
