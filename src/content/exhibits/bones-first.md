Nobody has ever seen a living dinosaur, so scientists work from the outside in. They start with the **bones**. A fairly complete skeleton shows the basic body and how it stood, and experts check that against footprints found in rock.

Next come the **muscles**. Muscle is rarely preserved, but the scars where muscles attached to the bones show where they were. Living relatives such as crocodiles help fill in the detail.

Last of all comes the **skin**. It sometimes fossilises, and bony plates in some armoured dinosaurs show what their skin was like. Where skin has not survived, scientists make a careful guess.

> **Look closer:** A dinosaur picture is a best estimate, built layer by layer. The bones are the most certain part, and the skin is the least.
