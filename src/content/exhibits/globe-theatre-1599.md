The first Globe Theatre was built in 1599 on Bankside, on the south bank of the Thames in London. It was built by the Lord Chamberlain's Men, the company that performed Shakespeare's plays, and Shakespeare was one of its shareholders.

The company later became known as the King's Men.

A modern Globe was opened in 1997, near its original site on Bankside.

> **Look closer:** The first Globe was built by actors who owned a share of it. Shakespeare was one of them, so the company that performed his plays also owned the building.
