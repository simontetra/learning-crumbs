On 2 November 1936 the BBC began its first regular public television service from Alexandra Palace in north London. At the time it was called high-definition television.

The system chosen was Marconi-EMI's 405-line system. It won a contest against the Baird Company's system, and it was used for many years afterwards.

> **Look closer:** Early television was black and white only. Colour came much later, and every picture in this room began in grey.
