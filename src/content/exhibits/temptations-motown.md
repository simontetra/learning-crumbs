The Temptations were formed in Detroit in 1960. They began as the Elgins, from a merger of two groups, the Primes and the Distants.

They signed to Motown Records in 1961, and recorded almost exclusively for the label. Their first major hit was "The Way You Do the Things You Do", in 1964.

The group was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1989. In 2013 it received a lifetime achievement award from the Recording Academy. The source does not list any individual Grammy wins.

> **Look closer:** The Temptations and The Supremes both came out of Detroit and recorded for Motown in the same years. Two groups from one label can share a city and a sound without sharing a story.
