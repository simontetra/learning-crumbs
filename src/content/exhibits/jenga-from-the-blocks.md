Jenga was designed by Leslie Scott, who was born in Tanzania in 1955 and grew up in several African countries. Her family played a game called **Takoradi Bricks** in Accra, Ghana, by 1974. The blocks came from a carpenter in Takoradi, which gave the game its first name.

The game was first sold through small outlets in 1982, and launched at the London Toy Fair in January 1983. The name is Swahili for "build". Scott registered it as a trademark.

It reached North America in 1986, when a Canadian company took it up, and Hasbro released it under Milton Bradley in 1987. Mental Floss reports that more than 50 million copies have been sold.

> **Look closer:** Scott has said she receives about 20 percent of the royalties from the game she designed.
