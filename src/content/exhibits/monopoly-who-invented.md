Monopoly is one of the best-known board games in the world, but the name on the box is not the name of its first designer.

Elizabeth "Lizzie" Magie made **The Landlord's Game** to teach people about land and rent. Her patent was granted in January 1904. Players who owned land collected rent from everyone else, and the game showed how quickly one person could get rich while the others got poorer.

Over the next thirty years the game was copied, changed and played in many homes. In 1933 Charles Darrow copyrighted a version and sold it as **Monopoly**. Parker Brothers bought the game from him in March 1935, and later bought Magie's older patent as well.

> **Look closer:** Darrow was long credited as the inventor. Parker Brothers helped build that story, and Magie's part only became widely known later.
