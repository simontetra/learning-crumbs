A bungalow is a small house, usually on one storey. The word comes from the Hindi word *banglā*, which means "of Bengal". Dictionaries describe it as a house built in the Bengal style.

Dictionary.com describes a British bungalow as a one-storey house, sometimes with an attic. Wikipedia describes a bungalow as a small house or cottage, usually one storey or one and a half storeys.

> **Look closer:** A word from one region of India became the name of a style of house, now used in many countries.
