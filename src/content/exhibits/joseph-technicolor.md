*Joseph and the Amazing Technicolor Dreamcoat* was first performed at Colet Court School in London on 1 March 1968. It was a short pop cantata of about fifteen minutes, written by Andrew Lloyd Webber and Tim Rice in 1967 for the school.

It was later performed at Westminster Central Hall, and by November 1968 had grown to about thirty-five minutes. A full-length version had been written by 1974.

The story comes from the Bible, from Genesis 37 to 50. The show opened off-Broadway in 1981, then at the Bernard B. Jacobs Theatre on Broadway in 1982. It closed in 1983 after 747 performances.

> **Look closer:** The school version is the start of a story that changed many times. Each stage of its growth has its own date, so check which version you are describing.
