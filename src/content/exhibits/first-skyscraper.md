The Home Insurance Building in Chicago was built in 1884 and 1885. It rose ten storeys, and it was later raised to twelve. Its weight was carried by a skeleton of iron and steel rather than by thick walls of stone or brick. That is why it is often called the first skyscraper.

> **Look closer:** Not everyone agrees on what counts as a skyscraper, so other buildings are sometimes given the title. The Home Insurance Building is the one most often named.
