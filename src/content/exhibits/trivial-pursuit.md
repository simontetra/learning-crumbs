Trivial Pursuit was invented in Montreal on 15 December 1979 by Chris Haney and Scott Abbott, two Canadian journalists. They spent roughly two years writing about 6,000 questions for the first game.

It was first sold in Canada in 1981, and in the United States in 1982.

> **Look closer:** The idea of a quiz board game, in which players answer questions about everything from history to sport, was new when this one arrived.
