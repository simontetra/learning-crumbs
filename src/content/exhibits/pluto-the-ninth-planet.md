Pluto was found in 1930 by **Clyde Tombaugh**, an astronomer working at Lowell Observatory in Flagstaff, Arizona. The observatory had hired him in 1929 to continue a search for a ninth planet that Percival Lowell had begun.

On **18 February 1930**, Tombaugh found a small object that moved against the background stars. He worked there as an observer until 1945.

> **Look closer:** Pluto was found by a deliberate search of the sky, not by chance. It was looked for as a planet, and it was counted as one for 76 years.
