Draughts grew out of an older game called alquerque. Carvings of alquerque date from about 1400 BC, and boards from about 600 BC.

The modern game was probably invented in southern France in the 12th century, on an eight-by-eight board. The game was called fierges, and the pieces ferses. In the 15th century the name changed to *jeu de dames*, and then simply dames.

The oldest known book about draughts was published in Valencia, Spain, in 1547.

> **Look closer:** Draughts is called checkers in America. Both names are for the same game, and the names changed as the game travelled between countries.
