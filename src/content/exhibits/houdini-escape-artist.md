Harry Houdini was born in Budapest, Hungary, on 24 March 1874, according to Britannica. His family moved to America when he was a child, and as a young man he performed as a trapeze artist under the name "Ehrich, the Prince of the Air". His family moved to America when he was a child, and as a young man he performed as a trapeze artist under the name "Ehrich, the Prince of the Air".

He began studying magic in his teens, and took the name Harry Houdini after the French magician Jean Eugène Robert-Houdin.

He was known for escapes from handcuffs, chains, crates and a water torture cell. In 1916 he freed himself from a straitjacket while hanging upside down from a building, watched by about 100,000 people. He died in Detroit on 31 October 1926, after a ruptured appendix, following a punch to the stomach that was meant to test his strength.

The sources do not agree on every detail. They give his birth name as Erik Weisz in some places and as Ehrich Weiss in others. Biography.com dates his Water Torture Cell to 1912, while PBS dates it to 1913.

> **Look closer:** Houdini's escapes were a test of nerve, planning and showmanship. Audiences came to see him get out of the impossible, and he made sure that they did.
