Guinness World Records recognises Elvis Presley as the best-selling solo artist in the United States. The record is based on 134.5 million units certified by the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA), and the recognition dates from 27 November 2014.

Sales figures for music are counted in different ways in different countries and at different times, so this is the figure used by Guinness, rather than a count of every record ever sold.
